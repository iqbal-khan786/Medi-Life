Medi Life - Hospital Management System

A full-stack hospital operations platform featuring dual role-based portals for patients and hospital administration.

Features:
Patient Portal - Self-registration, intake forms, live itemized billing, and high-resolution PDF invoice downloads.
Management Portal - Real-time bed allocation matrix, triage queues, and permanent patient ledger export (.xlsx).
Consultant Desk - Patient clinical lookup by ID/Name, assigned doctors, condition notes, and prescription management.
Government Card Scheme - Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY & Green Card ₹5 Lakh cashless coverage with persistent balance tracking.
